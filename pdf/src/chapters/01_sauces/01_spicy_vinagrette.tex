\subsection*{SPICY VINAIGRETTE}
\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{ingredients}
    \item 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
    \item 4 tbsp olive oil
    \item 1 clove garlic, very finely minced
    \item 1 fresh red chilli, finely diced
    \item 1/2 green pepper, very finely diced
    \item 1 ripe tomato, deseeded and finely diced
    \item 1/2 small onion, very finely diced
    \item Salt \& pepper to taste
\end{ingredients}
\end{multicols}
\subsection*{Instructions}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Combine the vinegar, olive oil, and garlic in a bowl and whisk to emulsify.
    \item Add the chilli, green pepper, tomato, and onion. Stir well.
    \item Season generously. Leave to stand for at least 15 minutes before serving so the vegetables soften slightly.
\end{enumerate}
