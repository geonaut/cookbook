\subsection*{POL SAMBOL}
\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{ingredients}
    \item 100g freshly grated coconut (or desiccated, rehydrated)
    \item 2 shallots, finely sliced
    \item 2 fresh red chillies, roughly chopped
    \item Juice of 1 lime
    \item 1/2 tsp salt
\end{ingredients}
\end{multicols}
\subsection*{Instructions}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Put all the ingredients into a pestle and mortar and pound until well combined — the coconut should be slightly broken down but still have texture.
    \item Taste and adjust: more lime for acidity, more chilli for heat, more salt if needed.
    \item Serve as a condiment alongside rice and curry.
\end{enumerate}
