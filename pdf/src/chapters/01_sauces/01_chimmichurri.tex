\subsection*{CHIMICHURRI}
\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{ingredients}
    \item Large bunch flat-leaf parsley, leaves only
    \item 1 tbsp dried oregano
    \item 3 cloves garlic, minced
    \item 1 fresh red chilli, finely diced (or 1/2 tsp chilli flakes)
    \item 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
    \item 6 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    \item Salt \& black pepper
\end{ingredients}
\end{multicols}
\subsection*{Instructions}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Finely chop the parsley by hand — don't use a blender or food processor, it turns it into a paste.
    \item Combine parsley, oregano, garlic, and chilli in a bowl.
    \item Stir in the vinegar and olive oil. Season generously.
    \item Leave to rest for at least 30 minutes before serving. The flavours improve significantly with time.
\end{enumerate}
