

\section{Chimmichurri}
Ingredients: garlic, parsley, oregano, red wine vinegar, olive oil, salt, pepper
Instructions: Chop all ingredients finely and mix together. Adjust seasoning to taste. Serve with grilled meats.

\section{Spicy vinagrette}
Ingredients: red wine vinegar, olive oil, garlic, fresh chilli, green pepper, fresh tomato, onion, salt, pepper
Instructions: Combine all ingredients in a bowl and whisk until well mixed. Adjust seasoning to taste.
