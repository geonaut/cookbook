\section{Bechamel Sauce}
\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{itemize}
    \item 50g butter
    \item 50g all-purpose flour
    \item 500ml milk
    \item Salt to taste
    \item Nutmeg (optional)
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Instructions}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item In a saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat.
    \item Add the flour and stir continuously for about 2 minutes to create a roux.
    \item Gradually whisk in the milk, ensuring there are no lumps.
    \item Continue to cook, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens (about 5-10 minutes).
    \item Season with salt and a pinch of nutmeg if desired.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Recipe Hints}
\begin{itemize}
    \item For a richer flavor, use whole milk or add a bit of cream.
    \item Be sure to whisk continuously to prevent the sauce from burning or sticking to the pan.
    \item This sauce can be used as a base for many other sauces, such as cheese sauce or Mornay sauce.
\end{itemize}
