\chapter{Desserts}

\section{Chocolate Mousse}
\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{itemize}
    \item 200g dark chocolate
    \item 3 eggs
    \item 50g sugar
    \item 300ml heavy cream
    \item A pinch of salt
    \item test
    \item test
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Instructions}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Melt the dark chocolate in a heatproof bowl over simmering water. Allow to cool slightly.
    \item In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs and sugar until pale and fluffy.
    \item Fold the melted chocolate into the egg mixture.
    \item In another bowl, whip the heavy cream until soft peaks form, then gently fold it into the chocolate mixture.
    \item Spoon the mousse into serving dishes and refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Recipe Hints}
\begin{itemize}
    \item For a richer flavor, use high-quality dark chocolate with at least 70\% cocoa.
    \item You can add a splash of vanilla extract for extra flavor.
    \item Serve with whipped cream or fresh berries for garnish.
\end{itemize}
