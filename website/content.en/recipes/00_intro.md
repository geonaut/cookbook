# My Approach to Cooking

I believe that cooking should be simple, accessible, and delicious. Every recipe in this collection has been tested and refined to be straightforward enough for a weeknight dinner, yet satisfying enough for special occasions.

My cooking style draws inspiration from various cuisines and traditions, with a focus on:

- **Quality ingredients**: Simple, fresh ingredients make the best dishes
- **Clear instructions**: Each recipe is written to be easy to follow
- **Flexibility**: Most recipes can be adapted based on what you have on hand
- **Flavor**: Bold, interesting flavors without unnecessary complexity

Whether you're a seasoned cook or just starting out, I hope you'll find something here that inspires you to get in the kitchen.

Happy cooking!
