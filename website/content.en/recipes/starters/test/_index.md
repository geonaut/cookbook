{{< instructions >}}
1. Clean the mushrooms and slice them thinly.
2. Melt butter in a pan and add crushed garlic.
3. Sauté mushrooms until golden brown.
4. Deglaze with wine and garnish with parsley.
{{< /instructions >}}
{{< ingredients >}}
- 500g Mushrooms
- 4 cloves Garlic
- 50g Butter
- Fresh Parsley
- Salt and Pepper
- Splash of White Wine
{{< /ingredients >}}
