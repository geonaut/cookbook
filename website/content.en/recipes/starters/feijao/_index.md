{{< instructions >}}
1. Pressure cook the beans and Bay Leaves for 1 hour, or less if pre-soaked.
2. Sauté the garlic and lardons in olive oil until fragrant.
3. Add the sautéed mixture to the cooked beans and pressure cook for a further 15 minutes.
4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
{{< /instructions >}}
{{< ingredients >}}
- Black turtle beans
- Garlic
- Lardons
- Bay leaves
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Pepper
{{< /ingredients >}}
{{< /recipe-grid >}}

---
### Hints & Tips
- Any pork sausage or smoked meat can be used instead of lardons.
- Don't overcook the beans; they should be tender but not mushy.
- Don't add too much water, otherwise the beans will be soupy.
