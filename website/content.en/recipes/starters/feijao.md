# Feijão

# Ingredients

* Black turtle beans
* Garlic
* Lardons
* Bay leaves
* Olive oil
* Salt
* Pepper

# Instructions

1. Pressure cook the beans and Bay Leaves for 1 hour, or less if pre-soaked.
2. Sauté the garlic and lardons in olive oil until fragrant.
3. Add the sautéed mixture to the cooked beans and pressure cook for a further 15 minutes.
4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
